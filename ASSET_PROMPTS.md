# XENedion — Digital Asset Production & Art Direction Prompts

This document records the exact art direction, generation parameters, and specifications used to generate the high-resolution, photorealistic corporate editorial imagery integrated into the **XENedion** Enterprise Resource Planning platform website.

---

## 1. Primary Brand Identity

- **File**: `public/assets/xenedion-logo.png`
- **Origin**: Original approved high-resolution brand asset provided directly by the client.
- **Visual Description**: Interlocking multi-chromatic woven ribbon motif (cyan `#00DDFC`, electric blue `#1264F5`, vivid orange `#FF642C`, purple `#7024E8`) followed by the geometric custom wordmark "xenedion" in deep primary navy (`#091F40`) with an orange square accent above the letter "i".
- **Rules**: Used unaltered, un-cropped, and un-distorted. Preserved on high-contrast clean backgrounds to guarantee wordmark legibility.

---

## 2. Editorial Image 1 — Connected Business Operations

- **Asset Path**: `public/assets/images/connected-operations.jpg`
- **Placement**: Hero Section & Connected Business Operations visual storytelling area.
- **Resolution**: 2048 x 1152 px (16:9 widescreen editorial ratio).
- **Art Direction Prompt**:
  > *"A high-resolution, photorealistic, professional editorial business photograph: A sophisticated modern European or American enterprise workplace with real professionals collaborating across sales, customer management, and operations. High-ceilinged contemporary glass architectural interior with natural diffused daylight, large windows, sleek wooden conference table and collaborative standing desk area. Professionals (diverse business team in clean, smart-casual modern executive attire) reviewing integrated data and operational plans together. Natural warm and crisp lighting, authentic expressions, realistic skin textures, sharp focus, Leica 35mm f/2.0 look, cinematic commercial editorial photography for a premier enterprise tech company. No glowing sci-fi holograms, no fake floating screens, no cartoonish elements, no deformed hands, completely realistic corporate setting."*
- **Role in Layout**: Anchors the right-hand side of the asymmetric hero composition, contrasting with the editorial heading to instantly convey enterprise credibility, teamwork, and modern operational synchronization.

## 3. Editorial Image 2 — Procurement & Business Coordination

- **Asset Path**: `public/assets/images/procurement-coordination.jpg`
- **Placement**: About XENedion Section split layout.
- **Resolution**: 1920 x 1280 px (3:2 documentary ratio).
- **Art Direction Prompt**:
  > *"A high-resolution, photorealistic, professional editorial business photograph: A modern corporate procurement and supply chain planning meeting. Two or three enterprise supply chain managers (smart business attire) reviewing supply routes, supplier schedules, and procurement contracts on a sleek wooden table with architectural tablets and modern documentation. Natural daylight coming through large industrial warehouse-loft windows overlooking a modern logistics hub. Confident, focused, authentic human expressions, natural skin tones, depth of field, 50mm f/1.8 aesthetic, high-end commercial editorial style for a corporate Fortune 500 annual report or enterprise software publication. Strictly realistic, no fake sci-fi HUDs or floating holograms, no CGI artifacts."*
- **Role in Layout**: Accompanies the detailed narrative of About XENedion, grounding the company's mission in tangible human coordination between departments.

## 4. Editorial Image 3 — Inventory & Fulfillment

- **Asset Path**: `public/assets/images/inventory-fulfillment.jpg`
- **Placement**: Why XENedion? Interconnected Business Functions section (Sales, Procurement, Inventory, Fulfillment).
- **Resolution**: 1920 x 1280 px (3:2 documentary ratio).
- **Art Direction Prompt**:
  > *"A high-resolution, photorealistic, professional editorial business photograph: A clean, state-of-the-art modern enterprise fulfillment and distribution center. An operations director and a logistics manager (wearing high-visibility vests over professional attire) reviewing real-time fulfillment flow, inventory organization, and dispatch operations. Clean, high-bay automated warehouse aisles with tidy pallet racking in soft background bokeh, natural skylight mixed with warm industrial architectural LED lighting. Believable operational details, authentic human collaboration, crisp focus, cinematic commercial documentary style. Realistic human anatomy and hands, no artificial holograms or fantasy elements."*
- **Role in Layout**: Provides a tangible visual anchor for the fulfillment, stock availability, and physical inventory flow discussed in the "Because Every Part of Your Business Matters" section.
